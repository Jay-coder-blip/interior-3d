Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

The short version
  • We use your room photos and scans only to build your room in 3D and design it.
  • Video scans go to our 3D processing partner, KIRI Engine. We delete our copy of the video once it has been handed over.
  • If you connect Pinterest, we only read your pins and boards to suggest room ideas. We never post or change anything.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Room photosYou take or upload quick-capture photosTo lay out your room in 3D
Room video scanYou record or upload a video scanTo build an exact 3D model of your room
Room detailsYou enter room size and confirm furnitureTo size the 3D room and designs correctly
Your design answersYou describe the room you want and pick moods, style and budgetTo generate designs that suit you
Pinterest pins and boardsYou connect PinterestTo find room ideas that match your description
Saved designs and productsYou save a design, version or productSo you can come back to them

We don't ask for your name, phone number, address or payment details. You can optionally add a first name so the app can greet you.

How we use it

  • To build a 3D version of your room.
  • To generate design concepts, colours and product suggestions for that room.
  • To show similar products from retailers. Product results in the current version are demonstration data, and we don't send your data to retailers.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train third-party models.

Video scans and KIRI Engine

When you choose a video scan, your video is sent securely to KIRI Engine, which turns it into a 3D model of your room. Our server deletes its copy of the video as soon as KIRI Engine has received it. We keep the finished 3D model so you can view and design with it.

KIRI Engine processes the video under its own terms and retention rules. Scans show the inside of your home, so avoid filming people, documents or anything you'd rather keep private.

Pinterest

Connecting Pinterest is optional. If you connect, you sign in on Pinterest and grant read-only access (boards:read, pins:read, user_accounts:read). We use it to:

  • search for room ideas that match what you describe, and
  • read pins from your own boards and rank them against your description.

We read the colours of the pins you choose to inspire your design. We never create, edit or delete pins or boards, and we don't store your Pinterest password. The access token is stored on the Interior 3D server so you stay connected. You can disconnect from the Ideas screen at any time, and you can remove access from your Pinterest account settings.

Where data is stored

  • On your device: quick-capture photos, your rooms, design answers, designs and saved products are kept in your browser's local storage.
  • On the Interior 3D server: finished 3D models from video scans, and your Pinterest connection.
  • With KIRI Engine: scan videos while they are processed, under KIRI Engine's terms.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose Share. A shared design includes the 3D preview, style, moods and product list. It never includes your original photos or scan video.

How long we keep it

  • Scan videos: deleted from our server once handed to KIRI Engine.
  • 3D models: kept until you delete the room.
  • Browser data: kept until you delete it in the app or clear your browser data.
  • Pinterest connection: kept until you disconnect.

Your choices

  • Delete a room, with its photos and designs, from the room's page.
  • Delete all room photos, or turn off “Keep my original photos”, in Profile.
  • Reset everything in Profile to remove all app data from your device.
  • Disconnect Pinterest from the Ideas screen.

Children

Interior 3D is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.
