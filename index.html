Interior design app

Redesign the room you already have.

Interior 3D scans your real room in 3D, finds ideas on Pinterest that match how you want it to feel, and designs new looks around your actual walls, window and furniture. Then it shows you where to buy each piece.

In development · private beta

How it works

  1. Scan your room

    Walk around it with your phone for a minute or two. We rebuild it in 3D exactly as it is.

  2. Describe the feeling

    Say what you want in your own words: calm, cozy, bold, like a boutique hotel.

  3. Pick ideas from Pinterest

    We show room ideas that match your description. You choose the ones you love.

  4. Explore designs in 3D

    Three concepts built around your space, in colours taken from your picks. Tap anything to change it.

  5. Shop the look

    Find similar pieces from Nigerian and international stores, and keep a shopping list.

How we use Pinterest

Connecting Pinterest is optional. When you connect, Interior 3D reads pins that match the room you describe, from Pinterest search or from your own boards, so the designs start from ideas you already like.

What we never do

  • We never create, edit or delete pins or boards.
  • We never post to Pinterest on your behalf.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can disconnect at any time.
